About The Festival

A Catalyst for Technological Excellence

Learn about our legacy, mission, and what awaits guests, participants, and industry leaders at this premier technology summit.

Fostering Ingenuity, Code & Engineering

INNOVA TECH FEST 2026 is the flagship national symposium hosted annually by [COLLEGE NAME]. Now in its 12th landmark edition, the festival unites over 3,000 curious tech minds, coders, roboticists, startup visionaries, and researchers under one high-energy campus roof.

The event bridges the divide between classroom theory and real-world industrial innovation. Through competitive coding, 24-hour hackathons, combat robotics, hardware exhibitions, and executive masterclasses led by Silicon Valley and ISRO scientists, participants test their limits, win lucrative cash rewards, and network with leading tech scouts.

⚡

Who Can Participate?

Open to undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma students from any college or university across India.

🎯

Main Objectives

To inspire problem-solving, promote open-source collaboration, and incubate impactful student startups.

🚀

What to Expect?

24+ technical events, hands-on AI workshops, live gaming arenas, startup pitches, and cultural neon nights.

🏛️

Host Institution

Organized by Department of Computer Science & Engineering with modern lab infrastructure.
